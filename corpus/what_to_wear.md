# What to wear in London

A short, practical guide used as supporting text for the "ask the forecast" feature.

## Rain

When rain is likely, a waterproof jacket with a hood beats an umbrella, which struggles in
London's gusty streets. Shoes that cope with puddles make a wet commute far more bearable.

## Wind

Strong gusts make it feel colder than the temperature suggests. A windproof outer layer
helps more than an extra jumper, and umbrellas are best left at home on windy days.

## Cold

When it feels cold, layers work better than one thick coat: you can take one off on the
Tube. A hat and gloves make a large difference on early mornings and late evenings.

## Mild

On mild days a light jacket or a jumper is usually enough, but evenings cool down quickly,
so carry an extra layer if you will be out after sunset.

## Warm

On warm days choose breathable clothing, carry water, and remember sun protection when the
UV index is moderate or high, even if it is breezy.

## Running

For a run, dress as if it were a little warmer than it feels: you will warm up quickly.
After dark wear something reflective, and on wet days pick a route with good footing,
such as the Thames Path's paved sections.
